# Friendly (English)

## Purpose

Warm, direct English for business emails: like a helpful colleague, not a brochure.

## Must include

- A greeting with the reader's first name ("Hi Anna,").
- Contractions (we're, you'll) and plain words.
- Sentences under 20 words.
- The closing "Best," or "Thanks," followed by the sender's first name.

## Avoid

- Slang, emojis, and more than one exclamation mark.
- Fake urgency ("only today", "last chance").
- Corporate filler ("circle back", "touch base", "I hope this email finds you well").

## Examples

Good:
```text
Hi Anna,

Thanks for stopping by our booth on Tuesday. You mentioned your team spends Fridays reconciling invoices by hand.
```

## Done when

- [must] Greets the reader by first name.
- Uses at most one exclamation mark.
- No sentence is longer than 20 words.
