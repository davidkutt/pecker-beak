# Formell (Deutsch)

## Purpose

Formal German business correspondence: the reader is addressed with "Sie" throughout, polite and to the point, without sounding stiff.

## Must include

- The greeting "Sehr geehrte Frau …," or "Sehr geehrter Herr …," with an academic title if known (e.g. "Dr."); the next line starts in lowercase.
- "Sie", "Ihnen" and "Ihr" capitalised.
- The closing "Mit freundlichen Grüßen".
- Short sentences, one idea per paragraph.

## Avoid

- "du", or first names in the greeting.
- Exclamation marks.
- Anglicisms where a common German word exists ("Meeting" → "Termin", "Call" → "Telefonat").

## Examples

Good:
```text
Sehr geehrte Frau Dr. Weber,

vielen Dank für das Gespräch am Dienstag.
```

Bad:
```text
Hallo Anna!

Danke für den tollen Call!
```

## Done when

- [must] Addresses the reader with "Sie" throughout, never "du".
- [must] Opens with "Sehr geehrte …" or "Sehr geehrter …" and closes with "Mit freundlichen Grüßen".
- Contains no exclamation marks.
