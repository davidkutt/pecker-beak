# B2B software (SaaS)

## Purpose

Writing to buyers of business software: team leads, operations and IT. They get many vendor emails and decide on concrete outcomes.

## Must include

- One measurable outcome (time saved, errors avoided, cost) instead of a feature list.
- The reader's role or team, so the outcome is clearly theirs.

## Avoid

- Buzzwords ("game changer", "synergy", "revolutionize").
- Feature lists of more than three items.
- Security certifications or integrations that the input or context does not state.

## Examples

## Done when

- States one measurable outcome relevant to the reader's role.
- [must] Mentions no certifications or integrations that the input or context does not state.
