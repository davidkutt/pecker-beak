# Pharma

## Purpose

Writing to buyers in pharmaceuticals: hospital pharmacies, pharmacies, wholesalers and procurement teams. They are regulated, risk-averse and short of time.

## Must include

- Reliability and compliance before novelty: supply security, GMP/GDP, documentation.
- Precise product names and strengths (e.g. "Metformin 500 mg") when products are mentioned.

## Avoid

- Health claims, or statements about the efficacy or safety of medicines.
- Marketing superlatives ("the best", "unbeatable").
- Mentioning patients or patient cases.

## Examples

## Done when

- [must] Makes no health, efficacy or safety claims about medicines.
- Names products with substance and strength, if it names products at all.
