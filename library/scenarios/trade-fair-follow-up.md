# Trade fair follow-up

## Purpose

A follow-up email after a short conversation at a trade fair, where the lead asked for information in writing. Tests whether the agent uses what was said and keeps the promise.

## Must include

- A reference to the conversation (where and when).
- The concrete problem the lead mentioned, in his own terms.

## Avoid

- Re-introducing the company as if the lead had never met the sender.
- Problems the lead did not mention.

## Examples

## Done when

- [must] Refers to the conversation at the trade fair.
- Addresses the problem the lead named (missed ibuprofen deliveries).
